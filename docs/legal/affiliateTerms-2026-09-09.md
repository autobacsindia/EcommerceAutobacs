## 2. Applying and being accepted

You become an affiliate only when we notify you that you have been approved and issue

You must be at least 18 years old and resident in India.

## 3. Your code and your link

On approval we issue you a single affiliate code. It works two ways:

- as a **discount code** your audience can enter at checkout; and
- as a **tracking link** (`?ref=YOURCODE`), which records a referral for **30 days**
  after someone clicks it.

If a customer enters a different affiliate's code at checkout, that affiliate is credited
instead of you, regardless of any earlier click.

You may promote us on your own website, channel or social accounts. You may **not**:

- use spam, malware, misleading redirects, or any automated traffic generation.

## 4. Commission

### 4.1 What you earn

Your commission rate is agreed individually with you when you are approved and is shown
on your affiliate dashboard. It is a percentage of the **net goods value** of a qualifying
order — that is, the order subtotal after any discount, and **excluding delivery charges
and taxes**.

Unless we agree otherwise in writing, commission is payable only on a customer's **first
order**.

### 4.2 When it is confirmed

Commission on an order becomes payable only after **all** of the following:

1. the order has been paid for and the payment has cleared;
2. every item has been delivered; and
3. the return window for that order has closed with no return outstanding.

Until then it shows on your dashboard as *pending*. Commission on an order that is
cancelled, returned or refunded is reversed in full or in proportion to the amount
refunded.

### 4.3 If an order is refunded after you have been paid

If an order is refunded after we have already paid you commission on it, the reversed
amount is carried as a negative balance and set against your future earnings. We will not
ask you to return money you have already received.

### 4.4 What does not earn commission

- Orders you place yourself, or that are placed using your own account, email address or
  phone number.
- Orders from anyone in your household, or any arrangement designed to route your own
  purchases through your code.
- Orders where the code was applied by our staff rather than by the customer.
- Orders that are cancelled, unpaid, fraudulent, or charged back.

## 6. Tax

You are an independent contractor. You are responsible for your own taxes.

### 6.1 Tax deducted at source (TDS)

We are required to deduct tax at source from commission payments. Our current
understanding is that affiliate commission falls under **Section 194H (commission or
brokerage)** of the Income-tax Act, 1961, and that deduction applies once your commission
in a financial year exceeds **₹20,000**.

Because of this, your **PAN is mandatory** to join the programme. Where PAN is not
available, a higher rate of deduction applies by law.

We will provide the certificate of deduction required for you to claim credit.

> **Note.** The section, rate and threshold above reflect our present understanding and
> are being confirmed with our chartered accountant. They may be corrected. We will apply
> the rate our accountant advises, and will tell you the rate applied on each payout.

### 6.2 GST

Your promotional services are a supply for GST purposes, which we understand falls under
**SAC 9983** at **18%**.

GST registration is **your** obligation, and generally arises only once your aggregate
turnover exceeds the registration threshold (₹20,00,000 for most persons and states). If
you are registered, you must give us your GSTIN and raise a proper tax invoice; we will
then pay GST in addition to your commission. If you are not registered, we pay commission
without GST.

This is why we ask for your address and state — place of supply for these services depends
on where you are located.
